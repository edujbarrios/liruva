# LIRUVA Code of Conduct

We want a welcoming community for musicians, beginners, maintainers, and developers.

Treat people with respect. Be considerate in technical discussions. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish private information. Assume good intent, but accept feedback and corrections.

Report inappropriate behavior privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub's reporting features or a private channel the maintainer publishes. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation to protect the community. Decisions should be fair and explained when possible.
